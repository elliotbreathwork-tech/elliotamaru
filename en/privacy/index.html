Privacy policy

This site does not measure your visit and sets no cookies itself. The booking calendar comes from Calendly, which applies its own rules. Here is what travels when you use the site, and what I do with the information you give me.

Who is responsible

Elliot Amaru, publisher of the site (see the legal notice). For any question about your data: elliot.breathwork@gmail.com.

What the site does with your visit

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. To send you the pages, GitHub receives your IP address and the technical details of your browser (GitHub privacy statement (opens in a new tab)). I have no access to these logs.

The site itself sets no cookies. It keeps two pieces of information in your browser: the fact that you passed the entrance screen (until the tab is closed) and the preview of my latest Instagram posts (twelve hours). They stay on your device and are never sent to me.

I use no audience measurement tool. The fonts are hosted on the site.

What loads from a third party

Booking calendar (Calendly). It is built into the home page and the session pages, and loads when you scroll down to it. As soon as it loads, Calendly receives your IP address and can set its own cookies. It then receives the information you enter to book (Calendly privacy policy (opens in a new tab)).

Instagram strip. To show my latest posts, your browser queries the Behold service and loads the images from its servers. Behold receives your IP address.

External links (Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, Calendly): their rules apply as soon as you click.

What I collect when you book or write to me

When you book, Calendly passes me your name, your email address and your answers. You then receive a health form (Google Forms), used to check that nothing stands in the way of the session. These answers are health data: I only use them with your explicit consent, to prepare the session.

When you write to me (email, WhatsApp, Instagram, phone), I use your message to reply to you.

The legal basis is carrying out your booking and answering your requests. For health data, it is your consent.

I keep this information as long as we are in touch, then for three years after our last exchange. After that, I delete it. I neither sell nor rent any data.

The information travels only between you, me and the services I use to receive it (Calendly, Google, WhatsApp, Instagram). Some are located in the United States: their privacy policies describe the safeguards applied to transfers.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for its correction or deletion, limit or refuse its use, take it back, or withdraw your consent at any time. Write to me at elliot.breathwork@gmail.com: I reply within a month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (opens in a new tab).
