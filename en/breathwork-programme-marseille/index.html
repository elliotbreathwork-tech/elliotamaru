Six weeks to find your breath again. And know how to use it.

You know those moments when you know you should slow down, but you can't quite manage it?

Your body is tired, your mind keeps spinning. The days run into each other, the tension builds up, and even when you finally have time for yourself, you struggle to switch off.

You don't necessarily need a new app, a complicated routine or an hour of meditation a day.

You can start with something you already have: your breath.

The Inner State Programme is six weeks to explore your breathing, learn to get through stress better and build a practice you can use in your daily life.

Not only during a session. Also on a Monday morning, before an important meeting, or in the evening, when your brain refuses to switch to silent mode.

Learning to breathe differently

We breathe about 20,000 times a day, often without even thinking about it. Yet the way we breathe can influence our physical and emotional state.

Slow, comfortable breathing can help you settle. Other practices, such as conscious connected breathing, let you explore your sensations and your relationship to emotions more deeply.

For six weeks, we take the time to try different techniques, to understand what works for you and to build a practice suited to your needs.

Four private sessions at the studio, guided exercises between appointments and a weekly follow-up.

The aim: that you gradually find your own bearings, without needing someone to guide you every time.

Six weeks to explore, integrate, anchor.

  1. Taking stockWe start with you: your daily life, your stress level, your sleep and what you would like to change. We draw up a first review, then explore conscious connected breathing. No prior practice needed, come as you are.
  2. Finding your toolsNot all breathing produces the same effects. We discover several short techniques, suited to different situations: slowing down after a busy day, getting back to calm or preparing the body for rest. You start to identify the ones that suit you.
  3. Bringing the practice into daily lifeBetween sessions, there is real life. Work, the unexpected, habits that take over again. We review your practice, what works and what is still hard to fit in. The idea is to find a rhythm that truly suits you.
  4. Going furtherWe deepen the breath and body work, at your own pace. If it fits your needs, we can also bring in an abdominal approach inspired by Chi Nei Tsang. Nothing is forced: we stay attentive to your sensations.
  5. Refining your practiceYou are starting to know your bearings better. We identify the exercises that are most relevant for you and adjust your routine. Ten minutes you actually take for yourself are worth more than a perfect routine dropped after four days.
  6. Reviewing and carrying on aloneWe go back to the indicators from the start and look at what has changed. You leave with a personalised ten-minute routine, guided exercises and bearings to keep practising on your own.

Support that goes beyond the session

The work isn't limited to the time spent at the studio.

Every week, we stay in touch by message to check in on your experience and your practice. You also have guided exercises to keep going at home, at your own pace.

The aim isn't to depend on permanent support, but to develop resources you can draw on yourself.
