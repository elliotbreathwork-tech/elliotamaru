How it works

I guide shamanic ceremonies in person, one on one.

The medicines I use are non-toxic, non-addictive, 100% organic and natural, not altered by humans.

We always start with a phone call, followed by a meeting.

This meeting is for getting to know each other: your path, your intentions, what you are looking for. It also checks that the approach suits you and that we both feel it is aligned. If so, we prepare the ceremony together, along with what comes before and after.

These are very deep, deeply true experiences that call for real preparation.

A ceremony opens a new perspective, widens awareness, sheds light on patterns that repeat, and can give you the drive to change something afterward.

Confidential setting. You decide what comes next.
