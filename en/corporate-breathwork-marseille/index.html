“Breathing under pressure” takes place on site, seated and fully dressed, for a group of 8 to 15 colleagues. Each participant leaves with two protocols to use during the day: one before a tense meeting, one in the evening to sleep.

The first call, 15 minutes long, sets the date, the number of participants, the place and the budget.

Professional liability insurance certificate, and a contraindications questionnaire filled in by each participant before the session.

1 hour, €450 for the workshop, all included. I travel within Marseille and the surrounding area.

  • AboutCertified by the International Breathwork Foundation after 300 hours of training, more than 2,500 people guided.
  • Location58 boulevard Bompard, 13007 Marseille
  • PrecautionA wellness practice, to be lived alongside your medical care. If you are pregnant, take medication for the heart or blood pressure, or live with epilepsy, glaucoma, poorly controlled asthma or a recent operation, talk to your doctor first.
