Group breathwork in Marseille, every Saturday at 10 am

Group breathwork brings everyone together on Saturday at 10 am, at 58 boulevard Bompard, Marseille 7th district. The session lasts 1 hour 30 and costs €50. Come as you are, for your first time or after years of practice. I guide the breath of the whole room, and many go on to a private session.
