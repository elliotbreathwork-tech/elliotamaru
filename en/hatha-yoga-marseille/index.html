What it is

I invite you to know why you do each posture and how your body settles into it.

We go back to the basics: stretches, breathing, alignment, asanas. Stability, breath, awareness of what moves.

Open to all levels, and valuable if you've been practicing for a while and want to strengthen your foundations.

You leave with what you need to practice alone at home.
