Frequently asked questions

Before booking

What is Hatha Yoga?

It is the yoga of postures and breath, practiced at a slow pace. Each posture, an asana, is held long enough to feel what stretches and where the breath moves. Breathing, the pranayama, guides the whole.

Do I need to be flexible to start?

You come with the body you have, and flexibility grows with practice. I adapt each posture: alignment first, depth after, according to what your body offers that day.

What is the point of a private class?

The whole class revolves around you: your pace and your questions. I correct an alignment as soon as it appears, and you feel the difference in your own body.

How are yoga and breathing connected?

The breath accompanies each posture and sets its rhythm. I am also a breathwork practitioner, and breathing is part of the class from the very first posture. To go further with it, there is the breathwork session in Marseille.
